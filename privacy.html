WorthIt: Real Cost

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Overview

WorthIt is designed to help you understand the personal cost of a purchase by converting its price into working hours and other illustrative figures.

WorthIt does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app is designed so that the personal financial information you enter remains on your device.

Information you enter

WorthIt may ask you to enter information including:

  • Monthly take-home pay
  • Monthly essential expenses
  • Working hours and working days
  • Items you are considering purchasing
  • Purchase prices
  • Your purchase decisions

This information is used to provide WorthIt's calculations, decision history, cooling-off list and progress features.

This information is stored locally on your device. WorthIt does not operate a user account system or cloud service for storing your financial profile or purchase decisions.

Notifications

If you choose the “Ask me again in 48 hours” feature, WorthIt may ask for permission to send notifications.

The reminder is scheduled locally on your device. You can control notification permission at any time through your device settings.

Usage information and analytics

WorthIt may use limited first-party technical or usage analytics provided through the app's development platform to help understand app performance and usage and to improve the service.

These analytics are not intended to include the take-home pay, purchase prices or purchase decisions that you enter into WorthIt.

WorthIt does not sell your personal information.

Deleting your information

WorthIt does not maintain a cloud account containing your financial profile or purchase history.

You can clear your WorthIt decision history from within the app. Removing the app from your device will remove locally stored WorthIt data associated with that installation, subject to your device and backup settings.

Financial information

WorthIt provides calculations and investment growth illustrations for informational purposes only.

WorthIt does not provide financial, investment or tax advice. Illustrated investment returns are not guaranteed and actual investment values can rise or fall.

Children

WorthIt is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WorthIt develops or if its features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
